Nutrition guide
Consistent meals built around your schedule and goals.
🥣
Breakfast options
Overnight oats — rolled oats + almond milk + chia seeds + cocoa powder + strawberries + 1 tbsp peanut butter. ~450 kcal. Best on Mon, Wed, Thu, Fri.
Eggs & avocado toast — 3 scrambled or poached eggs + 2 slices wholegrain toast + sliced avocado. High protein. Best on Tue, Thu.
Weekend breakfast — 3 eggs (any style) + wholegrain toast + avocado + black coffee. Relaxed pace.
🥗
Lunch formula
Protein (200g) — grilled chicken, turkey, salmon, tuna, shrimp or beef mince. Always.
Lift days — small brown rice or sweet potato. Bigger portion on leg day (Tue).
HIIT/rest days — skip rice or have a very small amount. Focus on salad and veg instead.
Always — generous vegetables or salad with olive oil and lemon dressing.
🥜
Snacks
Mid-morning (10am) — handful of mixed nuts + 1 apple, OR Greek yogurt + berries, OR 1 boiled egg.
Pre-workout (4:30pm) — 1 banana + black coffee. Before every training session.
Afternoon/weekend (3–4pm) — Greek yogurt (150g plain) + handful of walnuts or almonds.
🍽️
Dinner formula
Protein (180–200g) — salmon, cod, chicken breast, lean beef, chicken thighs.
Lift day dinners — roasted veg + small brown rice, quinoa, pasta, or wholegrain bread.
HIIT/rest day dinners — protein + steamed or roasted veg only. Skip carbs for fastest fat loss.
Friday treat meal — one meal of whatever you want. One meal, not a whole day.
Finish by 7:30pm — 2.5 hrs before bed for better overnight fat metabolism.
💊
Supplements — every day
Whey protein — 1 scoop post-workout in water. On rest days take with breakfast.
Creatine — 1 scoop daily. Post-workout on training days, with breakfast on rest days. Every single day — timing doesn't matter, consistency does.
Banana with shake — add a banana after lifting sessions (Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri). Skip after HIIT (Sun).
💧
Hydration
Daily baseline — 3 to 3.5 litres.
Training days — add 500ml during your session.
On waking — 500ml immediately. Kickstarts metabolism.
Desk strategy — finish 1L bottle by lunch, refill and finish again by 3:30pm.

Morning — every day
💧
500ml water on waking Always
Before coffee, before food. Rehydrates after overnight fasting. Put a full bottle next to your bed the night before.
🤸
5-min mobility routine Mon–Thu
Hip flexor stretch (60s each side), thoracic rotation (10 reps), shoulder circles (10 each). Reduces injury risk from sitting all day before an evening session.
During the work day
⏱️
Stand up every 60 minutes Work days
Set a phone reminder. Walk, take the stairs, do a lap. Sitting for 8 hours crushes your NEAT calorie burn.
📱
Walk during phone calls Work days
Any call without a screen — stand and walk. 3–4 calls a day adds 1,000–2,000 steps for free.
At the gym
📝
Log every set Training
Use the Track tab above. Without logging, you'll plateau after 4–6 weeks. Progressive overload is the engine behind both muscle gain and fat loss.
⏱️
Time your rest periods Training
60–75 sec for isolation (curls, lateral raises). 90–120 sec for compounds (squats, press, rows, pull-ups).
💪
Brace your core every lift Always
On squats, presses, rows, pull-ups — brace as if you're about to take a punch. Turns every compound into a core exercise. This is how abs are actually built.
Evening — every day
🚶
10–15 min walk after dinner Always
Reduces post-meal blood sugar spike, directly lowering fat storage. One of the highest-return habits for belly fat specifically over 8–12 weeks.
🌙
No screens 30 min before bed Always
Poor sleep quality raises cortisol and ghrelin — both directly linked to belly fat retention even if diet and training are perfect.
😴
Same bedtime every night Always
Staying up late on weekends and waking early Monday is one of the most underrated causes of stubborn belly fat. Aim for consistent 10pm–10:30pm.
Weekly
🥗
Saturday meal prep Weekly
Prep 5 overnight oat jars (10 min). Batch-cook proteins. Without this the plan breaks down by Wednesday. This single habit makes the whole week effortless.
